## 2. Does This Sound Familiar

**Heading:**
Does this sound familiar?

**Body:**
You have patients who —

- Continue to struggle despite appropriate treatment
- Have seen multiple providers without lasting improvement
- Feel overwhelmed, discouraged, or unsure what to try next
- Have recovery barriers that don't fit neatly into a diagnosis or pain score

The challenge may not be identifying the pain. It may be understanding what's making recovery harder.

---

## 3. The Real Question

**Heading:**
Pain intensity asks one question. Recovery asks another.

**Body — left column or first block:**
Chronic pain assessment often answers:
- Where does it hurt?
- How intense is the pain?
- What is the diagnosis?

**Body — right column or second block:**
But when recovery stalls, the more useful questions are often about:
- Flare patterns
- Function
- Confidence
- Healthcare experiences
- Daily life
- Recovery readiness

**Closing line:**
Recovery isn't shaped by pain alone.

## 4. Introducing the PCST

**Heading:**
The Pain Complexity Screening Tool™

**Body:**
The PCST is a pilot-stage framework designed to help organize the factors that may be making recovery more difficult into meaningful domains — supporting clearer clinical conversations and more individualized care planning.

It is intended to support, not replace, clinical judgment.

## 5. How It Works

**Heading:**
A Structured Approach to Pain Recovery Conversations

**Step 1 — Free Provider Consultation**
We'll talk about your practice, your patients, and whether the pilot is a good fit.

**Step 2 — Refer a Patient**
Eligible patients receive a free, nurse-led Pain Profile during the pilot — at no cost to you or your patient.

**Step 3 — Pain Profile**
Marisela personally conducts the Pain Profile conversation and organizes the findings using the PCST framework. A structured summary is shared with you, the referring provider, to support your own care planning.

**Step 4 — Care Planning**
The findings help support more individualized conversations about next steps with your patient.
