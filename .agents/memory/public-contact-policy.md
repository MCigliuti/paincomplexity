name: Public contact policy
description: Why PainComplexity.com uses scheduling and inquiry forms instead of public telephone contact.

Do not display telephone numbers in public PainComplexity.com content. Use provider consultation scheduling and the professional inquiry form as the contact paths.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested removal of public phone contact details because of potential spam.

**How to apply:** Follow this policy when editing contact sections, footers, metadata, social-preview assets, or future pages. Do not restore legacy call-or-text copy. This project does not control the separate PainCareAtHome.com website.
